# Privacy Policy for Tiny Talker Effective date: 21 August 2026 Last updated: 21 August 2026 Tiny Talker is an early-learning app designed for toddlers and preschoolers. We respect the privacy of children and families and are committed to providing a safe learning experience. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Tiny Talker. ## Information We Collect Tiny Talker does not require users to create an account. We do not knowingly collect: * A child’s email address or telephone number * Precise location * Contacts * Photos, videos or voice recordings * Advertising identifiers * Payment-card information * Information for behavioural advertising * Information for tracking users across other apps or websites ## Child Profile Information A parent or guardian may enter a child’s first name or nickname, select an age group and choose an avatar to personalise the learning experience. This information is stored locally on the user’s device. It is not uploaded to our servers, shared publicly, sold or used for advertising. Parents can remove this information through the App’s settings or by deleting Tiny Talker from the device. ## Internet Access and Educational Content Tiny Talker may use an internet connection to download educational images, audio, app configuration and updated content. We may use Firebase to manage or synchronise educational content and Cloudinary to host and deliver images, audio and other media. These service providers may automatically process limited technical information, such as an IP address, request time and requested content, when necessary to deliver their services, maintain security or prevent misuse. We do not use this information to identify children, display behavioural advertising or track users across other apps or websites. ## Purchases Tiny Talker may offer in-app purchases that unlock additional content or features. All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store complete payment-card information. Apple may provide limited purchase information, such as the product purchased, transaction confirmation and purchase status, so that Tiny Talker can unlock or restore purchased content. Purchases and external links intended for adults are protected by a parental gate where required. ## Advertising and Tracking Tiny Talker does not display third-party advertisements. We do not sell personal information and do not use information for behavioural advertising or cross-app tracking. ## Third-Party Services Tiny Talker may use the following service providers: * Apple – App Store distribution, in-app purchases and purchase restoration * Firebase – app configuration and educational-content synchronisation * Cloudinary – hosting and delivery of educational images, audio and media These providers process information according to their own privacy policies. We do not authorise third-party providers to use children’s information for advertising, profiling or tracking. ## Children’s Privacy Tiny Talker is intended to be configured and supervised by a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information online directly from children. If we learn that a child’s personal information was collected contrary to this policy, we will take reasonable steps to delete it. A parent or guardian may contact us to: * Ask what information we hold * Request access, correction or deletion * Prevent further collection or use * Withdraw previously provided consent We may need to verify that the person making the request is the child’s parent or legal guardian. ## Data Retention and Deletion Child-profile information stored locally remains on the device until it is removed through the App or Tiny Talker is deleted. Our service providers may retain limited technical logs for the period necessary to operate their services, maintain security, prevent misuse and meet legal obligations. To submit a privacy or deletion request, contact us using the details below. ## Data Security We use reasonable technical and administrative measures intended to protect information handled through Tiny Talker. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## International Data Processing Our service providers may process limited technical information in countries other than the country where the user lives. Where required, information will be handled according to applicable privacy and data-protection laws. ## External Links Parent-facing sections of Tiny Talker may contain links to our support page, Privacy Policy or other external resources. External links intended for adults are protected by a parental gate where required. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external websites. ## Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when Tiny Talker’s features, information practices or legal requirements change. Any updated policy will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
