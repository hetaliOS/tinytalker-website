Tiny Talker

Learn, Listen and Play

Tiny Talker is a colourful early-learning app for toddlers and preschoolers. Children can explore first words through clear pictures, spoken names, familiar sounds and playful quizzes.

Discover animals, birds, fruits, vegetables, vehicles, colours, shapes, numbers, ABC, professions, body parts and more.
