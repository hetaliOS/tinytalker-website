# Tiny Talker Support Welcome to Tiny Talker Support. Tiny Talker is an early-learning app for toddlers and preschoolers. Children can explore first words using pictures, spoken names, sounds, autoplay activities and playful quizzes. ## Contact Us If you have a question, experience a technical problem or want to share feedback, please contact: Email: brightnestinnovation@gmail.com When reporting a problem, please include: * Your iPhone or iPad model * Your iOS or iPadOS version * The Tiny Talker app version * A short description of the problem * A screenshot, if possible Please do not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information. ## Frequently Asked Questions ### Why is there no sound? Please check that: * Your device volume is turned up. * Silent Mode is turned off. * Bluetooth audio is not connected to another device. * Tiny Talker has been closed and reopened. ### Why are some images or sounds not appearing? Some content may require an internet connection during its initial download. Check your connection, keep the app open while the download finishes and try again. ### Can Tiny Talker be used without an internet connection? Content that has already been downloaded may be available offline. An internet connection may still be required for initial downloads, purchases, restoration or updated content. ### How do I restore a previous purchase? Open Tiny Talker, go to Settings or the purchase screen and select “Restore Purchases.” Make sure the device is signed in with the Apple Account originally used for the purchase. ### How can I manage my child’s use of the app? A parent or guardian should configure the app and supervise its use where appropriate. You can also use Apple’s Screen Time and Guided Access features to manage access on your device. ### How do I request data deletion? Send your request to brightnestinnovation@gmail.com with the subject “Tiny Talker Data Deletion Request.” We may ask for limited information needed to identify the relevant app data. ## Feedback We welcome suggestions that can help improve Tiny Talker.
